It's been a year since I last updated my [default apps list](/blog/default-apps-2024/). The main driving force behind the update in 2025 is consistently the same app for multiple use cases and switching to Android from iOS as a primary device and eliminating some apps from my professional and personal workflows.

- 📨 **Mail Client:** Gmail
- 📮 **Mail Server:** [Google](https://www.google.com/gmail/about/)
- 📝 **Notes:** [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) + [Notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/notes/id1110145109)
- ✅ **To-Do:** [Notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/notes/id1110145109) + Pen and paper + Linear for work
- 📷 **Photo Shooting:** [Galaxy Z Fold 7](https://www.samsung.com/in/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold7/) Camera app
- 🟦 **Photo Management:** Locally backing up in external drive on monthly basis
- 📆 **Calendar:** [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/)
- 📁 **Cloud File Storage:** [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/)
- 📖 **RSS:** [Reader](https://readwise.io/read)
- 🙍🏻‍♂️ **Contacts:** Contacts app by Samsung
- 🌐 **Browser:** [Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/) professionally on desktop and Android, [Brave](https://brave.com/) and [Safari](https://www.apple.com/in/safari/) personal
- 💬 **Chat:** [Slack](https://slack.com/), [Telegram](https://web.telegram.org/)
- 🔖 **Bookmarks:** [Readwise](https://readwise.io/read)
- 📑 **Read It Later:** [Readwise](https://readwise.io/read)
- 📜 **Word Processing:** [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) + [Notion](https://www.notion.so/)
- 📈 **Spreadsheets:** [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com/)
- 📊 **Presentations:** (rarely) [Google Slides](https://slides.google.com/)
- 🛒 **Shopping Lists:** N/A
- 🍴 **Meal Planning:** N/A
- 💰 **Budgeting and Personal Finance:** N/A
- 📰 **News:** N/A
- 🎵 **Music:** YouTube Music
- 🎤 **Podcasts:** YouTube Music
- 🔐 **Password Management:** [1Password](https://1password.com/)
- 🧑‍💻 **Code Editor:** [Cursor](https://www.cursor.com/) I've found that I used Cursor more than VS Code, both for work and personal projects
- 👾 **Terminal Emulator:** [iTerm](https://iterm2.com/)
- 🌌 **(Annotated) Screenshots and quick videos:** [CleanShot X](https://cleanshot.com/)
- 🌤️ **Weather:** Weather by Samsung on Android

**Now, there is one category I want to add myself:**

- ✍️ **Blogging:** Built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and hosted on [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/)

This covers app the list of apps that I use almost daily either for work or for personal use.
